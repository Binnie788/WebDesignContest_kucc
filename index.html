Festivals of light, family and gratitude

Tihar

Tihar is Nepal’s five-day festival of lights. Homes glow with diyos, colourful bulbs and rangoli, and we honour the bond between people, animals and the divine.

  • Kag Tihar - crows, messengers of news
  • Kukur Tihar - dogs get a tika and garland
  • Gai Tihar & Laxmi Puja - cows, and lights to welcome Laxmi
  • Mha Puja / Govardhan Puja - self-worship and oxen
  • Bhai Tika - sisters bless their brothers

Chhath

Chhath is a Hindu festival of thanks to Surya, the Sun, and Chhathi Maiya. It is widely celebrated in Nepal’s Terai (Madhesh) and in Bihar, a few days after Tihar.

  • Devotees fast and stand in rivers or ponds
  • Arghya is offered at sunset and at sunrise
  • A bamboo soop holds fruits, sugarcane, thekua and a diyo
  • It celebrates nature, purity and community
